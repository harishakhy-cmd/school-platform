Laboratory Safety & GHS Hazard Standards
GHS Globally Harmonised Hazard Pictograms:
Essential Chemistry Lab Protocols:
Personal Protection (PPE): Safety goggles, lab coats, and closed shoes are mandatory at all times. Tie back long hair.
Acid Dilution Rule: Always add concentrated acid to water slowly with constant stirring. Never add water into acid.
Fume Hood Usage: Carry out reactions producing toxic gases (\(\text{Cl}_2, \text{NO}_2, \text{SO}_2\)) strictly inside a fume cupboard.
Chemical Spill Protocol: Wash affected skin immediately with copious running water for at least 15 minutes. Notify the tutor.
